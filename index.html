Applications

Where these machines fit.

Best fit for synthetic runs

Synthetic and filament production

Waterjet and high-efficiency Airjet systems can support high-speed weaving across synthetic and filament-heavy cloth requirements.

Best fit for cotton

Cotton and blended fabrics

Airjet configurations are often selected for cotton and blended weaving lines that need reliable speed and stable registration.
